## How we work

This is how a typical ISO 9001:2015 project runs, from the first meeting to your certification audit.

6 to 8 weeksTypical development

8 to 10 weeksTypical implementation

3 to 6 monthsRecommended from start to finish

### Pre-assessment and project plan

The project starts with a kickoff session, which can take a few hours or a full day. You assign a core team, and our consultants begin by understanding where your business is heading: its vision, mission and values. This helps us understand the context of your organization, your stakeholders (interested parties) and the internal and external issues you face. It also makes sure your leadership understands risk-based thinking, which then carries through the whole ISO 9001:2015 development and implementation project.

### Process flow charts

We use process mapping software to capture your processes as they work today, then improve them with input from teams across your organization. The improved flow charts meet ISO 9001:2015 requirements and are built to deliver better results. How many processes we map depends on how complex your organization is.

### Manual creation

We write the documented information you need (manuals, policies and procedures) based on your risks and requirements, building on the documentation you already have.

### Implementation coaching and support

How long this takes depends on your organization. Development usually takes 6 to 8 weeks, and implementation another 8 to 10. During this phase we turn your documented information into a working quality management system that conforms to ISO 9001:2015. We recommend finishing within 3 to 6 months so the project keeps its focus. Our consultants lead the development and prepare your team to lead the implementation, with our support whenever you need it.

### Internal audit, management review and pre-certification audit

We can train your staff as internal auditors, or run the internal audits ourselves. We facilitate your management review meeting so the right inputs are discussed and your decisions are backed by evidence. A record of the review, including its outputs, must be kept. When you are ready for certification, we carry out a pre-certification audit to confirm every required process is working. Our consultants also work as external auditors, so you go into your certification audit with confidence.

### External audit

We coach your team on working with the external auditors, with practical strategies that take the stress out of the day. We have a perfect record of successful registration. We guarantee you will pass your first ISO 9001:2015 audit if you follow our plan.

## What we ask of your leadership

Pioneer brings the expertise to develop and implement ISO 9001:2015, but your leadership team will be expected to:

- Assign roles and responsibilities for the quality management system

- Provide resources, and a war room for training, coaching and planning

- Make sure process owners take part in improving their processes

- Support our consultants throughout the ISO project
