Services

Consulting, training and auditing for ISO management systems

Consulting

Our consultants are business process experts with real experience across many sectors, and their focus is helping organizations perform better.

Because we implement management systems through process improvement, any new system adds value by making your organization more efficient and effective. Our consulting covers:

  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management system development and implementation
  • Upgrading a quality management system to ISO 9001:2015
  • ISO 14001:2015 environmental management system development and implementation
  • Upgrading an environmental management system to ISO 14001:2015
  • OHSAS 18001:2007 occupational health and safety management system development and implementation
  • Internal audit training
  • Internal auditing of management systems
  • Management system awareness training

Training

Our trainers are assessors and auditors with many years of experience auditing and maintaining management systems.

We train groups in a classroom setting or work with people one-to-one. Our training includes:

  • Internal audit training
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management system awareness training
  • ISO 14001:2015 environmental management system awareness training
  • OHSAS 18001:2007 occupational health and safety management system awareness training

Auditing

Our certified lead auditors can carry out your internal audits, often at a much lower cost than doing them in-house.

Clients can choose to start with internal audit training, then have their own auditors run the internal audits under the supervision of our lead auditors. We find this the most effective way to train auditors and get the audits done. By the following year, your auditors have the skills to run the audits on their own.

We can shape an auditing project around your organization’s needs. Contact the Pioneer team to talk it through.
